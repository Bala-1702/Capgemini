Automatic Attendance System USing Face Recognition
In my project, I developed an automatic attendance system using face recognition technology. The system utilizes OpenCV to access the camera and process live video feeds. It captures and reads the video in real-time.
For the face recognition aspect, I utilized a face recognition package to extract and identify facial encodings from the live video. These encodings are then matched against a database of stored images to verify and record attendance. This approach reduce manual effort and increase accuracy.And also it update the attendance along with time in excel.
 
Anti-Drowning Wristband
This anti-drowning wristband is designed to prevent drowning incidents by automatically inflating an airbag when the wearer falls into a water source. The wristband integrates several key components:
BMP 180 Pressure Sensor: Detects changes in atmospheric pressure to identify when the wearer is submerged in water.
Arduino: Acts as the central processing unit, interpreting data from the pressure sensor and triggering the airbag inflation.
GPS Module: Provides real-time location tracking of the wearer, ensuring quick and accurate rescue operations.
GSM Module: Sends emergency alerts with the wearer's location. 
Airbag: Rapidly inflates to keep the wearer afloat until help arrives.

To program the F3 Evo Flight Controller, you can follow these general steps:
Install Cleanflight Configurator:
Download and install the Cleanflight Configurator from the Chrome Web Store or the official Cleanflight website.
Connect the Flight Controller:
Use a micro USB cable to connect the F3 Evo Flight Controller to your computer.
Install Drivers:
Ensure you have the necessary drivers installed. For Windows, you may need to install the STM32 Virtual COM Port Driver.
Flash Firmware:
Open Cleanflight Configurator.
Select the correct COM port.
Go to the "Firmware Flasher" tab.
Choose the appropriate firmware for your F3 Evo (e.g., Cleanflight or Betaflight).
Click "Load Firmware [Online]" and then "Flash Firmware".
Configure Settings:
Once the firmware is flashed, go to the "Setup" tab.
Calibrate the accelerometer.
Configure the receiver settings (PPM, SBUS, DSM) based on your setup.
Set up the flight modes and other preferences.
Tune PID Settings:
Adjust the PID (Proportional, Integral, Derivative) settings to fine-tune the flight characteristics.
Save and Test:
Save your configuration.
Test the flight controller on your UAV to ensure everything is working correctly.
